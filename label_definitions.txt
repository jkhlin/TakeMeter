analysis: makes a point and backs it with a specific, checkable detail that the main claim depends on: a measurement, a date, a version, a named product, person or event, or something concrete the writer did or saw
hot_take: asserts a view about the world, a company, a person, or what should happen, and offers nothing checkable for the main claim; might be true, isn't argued
reaction: expresses the writer's feeling about the story (thanks, praise, sympathy, RIP, excitement, disgust) without asserting anything about causes, other parties, or what should happen
